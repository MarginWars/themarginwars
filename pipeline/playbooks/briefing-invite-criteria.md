# The Margin Wars Briefing: invite-list criteria (~150 seats, no names, draft)

Based on 07 §3 (invite rules, Chatham House, ~40 founding invites) and 10 rung 0.

## Qualifying test (all three)

1. **Owns a number:** directly accountable for a payment cost, approval, loss or FX line.
2. **Seniority:** Director+ (or equivalent at scale-ups owning the P&L line).
3. **Operator, not seller:** works at a merchant, marketplace, platform/PayFac, PSP/acquirer or bank in an operating role. Vendor sales/marketing, analysts-for-hire, press, recruiters: excluded. Vendor product/risk operators allowed up to a cap.

## Exclusions

Current colleagues at Avi's employer and any party where a conflict exists [CONFIRM WITH AVI]; direct client contacts; anyone asking to resell or quote content.

No single company >2 seats; no sector >35%.
