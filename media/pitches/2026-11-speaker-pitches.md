## 1. MPE 2027 speaker pitch

To: {{contact.email}}

Subject: Speaker proposal — MPE 2027 Berlin — Six payment moves that win back margin in a quarter

Body:

Hello,

Please accept this as a speaking proposal for MPE 2027, 9–11 March, Berlin.

Title: Six payment moves that win back margin in a quarter
I am Avi Reddy, Founder of The Margin Wars. Anitha Williams, co-author of The Margin Wars, is available as co-speaker. The session is a practical cost-and-margin briefing for merchants and PSPs, not a product pitch. It sits closest to your 2027 areas on merchant growth, conversion and payment performance, and on payment orchestration.

Abstract:
Payment cost is one of the few P&L lines an operator can move inside a quarter without shipping a new product. This session works through six moves, one at a time: price on net revenue and take rate, not headline TPV; choose agent or merchant of record flow by flow; quote the FX markup per corridor, per order; move one corridor to local rails or account-to-account; price the agent channel; and map licence and tax exposure before a flow gets big. Each move comes with its mechanics, the owner, the proof metric and the rollback trigger, plus how it changes across the UK/EU, the US, Africa and APAC. No product pitch.
Takeaways: (1) a one-page test for whether deals are priced on net margin or on volume; (2) how to find, per corridor, where the FX markup sits and who keeps it; (3) a move-by-move checklist: owner, proof metric and rollback trigger for each.

I have spoken at Snowflake Summit 2025, Money20/20 USA 2024, Fiserv Forum 2024 and MRC Las Vegas 2024, where I hosted "Marketplaces on the Rise."

Happy to use the speaker-proposal form instead if you would prefer we resubmit there.

Thank you,
Avi Reddy

Body:

Hello,

I would like to be considered for a speaking slot at Sibos 2027, 20–23 September, Marina Bay Sands, Singapore, including the main conference or the Discover Stage if that is the better fit.

Title: Price the rail, not the token: stablecoin settlement as a cost line for merchants and PSPs

Abstract:
Stablecoin settlement only helps a merchant or a PSP if it beats the rails already on the cost table — interchange, FX markup, correspondent fees and payout timing — corridor by corridor. This session treats stablecoin settlement as a cost and margin line, not a theme. It works through three moves: put issuance, on- and off-ramp, FX and payout cost next to card and account-to-account alternatives for one corridor; decide who carries the float, the reserve and the operational failure; and set a rollback trigger before volume moves. Each move is shown across the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC, including where licence, banking access or off-ramp liquidity makes the cheaper rail the wrong one. No product pitch.
Takeaways: (1) a one-corridor cost table that puts stablecoin settlement next to cards, local rails and correspondent payout; (2) who carries float, reserve and operational failure when settlement moves off traditional rails; (3) a go/no-go and rollback test before a corridor is switched.

I am Avi Reddy, Founder of The Margin Wars. Anitha Williams, co-author of The Margin Wars, is available as co-speaker. The session is a cost-and-margin briefing for banks, PSPs and merchants, not a product pitch. I have spoken at Snowflake Summit 2025, Money20/20 USA 2024, Fiserv Forum 2024 and MRC Las Vegas 2024, where I hosted "Marketplaces on the Rise."
