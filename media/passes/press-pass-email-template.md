# Press / media pass request: email template (DRAFT, nothing sent)
Drafted 11 Nov 2026 (PT) for Avi's approval. Updated 11 Nov 2026 (PT) evening with the press-pass track and the media-partner variants modelled on how The Payments Shed works (see wide_scan_1111.md §2 and §8). Send only after Avi's explicit OK, one event at a time, from the address Avi chooses. Many organisers use a web form instead of email (see the tracker); paste the same text into the form.

**Honesty rules for this template**
- Describe The Margin Wars as what it is: an independent payments-tactics site (themarginwars.com) with an invite-only monthly Briefing on Substack.
- Claim no subscriber numbers, audience size, impressions or traffic. If a form asks, write "New publication, launched November 2026; no audience figures to share yet."
- Link only to pages that are public at the time of sending. The Substack is currently private and unlisted, so do not link Substack posts until Avi makes them public.
- Disclose that the editor works in payments. Organisers decide whether that qualifies.

At [Event] I plan to [cover sessions on X and Y / interview speakers on Z / write a tactics recap for the Briefing]. Coverage would appear as [a post on themarginwars.com and in the Briefing] within [two weeks] of the event.

Recent writing:
