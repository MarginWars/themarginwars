## Public signals (week of 2026-11-09)

**Previous runs:** [week of 2 November 2026](page-content-2026-11-02.md) (run #2) · [week of 26 October 2026](page-content-2026-10-26.md) (run #1, baseline). Full sourced findings for this week, including the figures-in-drafts register, are in public-signals-2026-11-09.md.

1. **A quiet week for Issue 01.** No rule it relies on changed. The three dates that matter are all still ahead:
   - the US swipe-fee settlement hearing on 16 November;
   - the deadline to seek a rehearing of the debit-cap ruling, around 10 December on our reading;
   - the European Parliament's vote on the new payments rules, indicatively 14 December.

   On the settlement, one search engine's summary claimed the settlement had already been approved. It had not: the summary was built from the *proposed* order. The settlement site still says the court has not given final approval. [Settlement website](https://www.interchangeequitablereliefsettlement.com/) · [8th Circuit docket, No. 25-3000](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/circuit-courts/ca8/25-3000)
2. **Mastercard is replacing its merchant token service with Secure Card on File (SCOF) from 31 December 2026.** Merchants that use their own or a third-party tokeniser are told to confirm they are ready "by the end of the year". Issue 02 goes out five days after the switch and currently names the old service. [Checkout.com: Mastercard migration to SCOF](https://support.checkout.com/hc/en-us/articles/39222017788690-Mastercard-migration-to-Secure-Card-on-File-SCOF)
3. **A second debit-cap case is waiting on a decision.** The 6th Circuit heard *Linney's Pizza* on 22 October. If it upholds the Fed's rule, it would split from the 8th Circuit. An analyst quoted by American Banker expects the Fed to wait for that ruling before rewriting the cap. [CourtListener docket, No. 25-6038](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/72036766/linneys-pizza-llc-v-board-of-governors-of-the-federal-reserve-system/) · [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/court-ruling-makes-lower-swipe-fees-likely-not-imminent)
4. **In the UK, Visa and Mastercard must follow new governance rules when they set scheme fees, from the end of November 2026.** The PSR's final directions also require more fee transparency from July 2027. From 2028, schemes must give six months' notice of material fee changes, defined as above £250,000. We missed this in earlier runs. Separately, the bill that abolishes the PSR and moves its work to the FCA has its Commons second reading on 10 November. [PSR PS26/1](https://www.psr.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps261-market-review-of-card-scheme-and-processing-fees-final-decision-information-transparency-and-complexity-remedy-pricing-governance-remedy/) · [BRC summary](https://brc.org.uk/news-and-events/news/finance/2026/ungated/psr-issues-final-directions-to-card-schemes-covering-transparency-and-pricing-governance/) · [Commons Library CBP-10886](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10886/)
5. **The EU's new payments rules are now formally with the Parliament for its second reading.** The Council's published text confirms the regulation applies 21 months after it enters into force, with some provisions at 27 months. One secondary source still says 18/24 months, which is out of date. [EP Legislative Observatory](https://oeil.secure.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2023/0210(COD)) · [Council position, PSR (ST 8543/1/26 REV 1)](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-8543-2026-REV-1/en/pdf) · [Council position, PSD3 (ST 8535/1/26 REV 1)](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-8535-2026-REV-1/en/pdf)
6. **India's UPI merchant charge has passed Diwali without a start date, and it now faces a Supreme Court challenge.** NPCI defended the 0.4% charge in a 3 November affidavit but gave no date. Financial Express reports a possible 1 January 2027 start, citing unnamed sources. Separately, RBI's 2026 e-mandate rules (recurring debits without extra authentication up to ₹15,000, with 24 hours' notice) are now sourced directly. [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-npci-defends-upi-mdr-charges-supreme-court/) · [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/money/upi-mdr-after-diwali-when-will-charges-start-who-pays-and-what-changes-4378408/) · [RBI E-mandate Framework 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13374)
7. **Nigeria has published full stablecoin regulations.** Issuers need ₦10bn in capital for naira tokens and ₦15bn for foreign-currency tokens. Naira issuers must keep at least 20% of reserves at the central bank. We read the text from a rehosted copy because we could not find a link on cbn.gov.ng. [Regulation text (BitKE rehost)](https://bitcoinke.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/REGULATIONS-FOR-THE-OPERATIONS-OF-NON-SECURITY-VIRTUAL-ASSETS-IN-NIGERIA-BitKE.pdf) · [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/11/07/cbn-tightens-stablecoin-rules-sets-n15bn-capital-floor-for-dollar-tokens/)
8. **Unchanged:**
   - **Visa:** no new rules edition. The 24 October edition still allows 20 retries in 30 days.
   - **Network-token fees:** the Europe, Asia Pacific and UAE fees are as they were.
   - **Stablecoins:** no change to the US GENIUS rules or the Bank of England's draft code.
   - **South Africa:** no redraft of its crypto rules.

## Our approach

The approach itself has not changed. What has changed is where the space is open. A Checkout.com-sponsored Brainfood essay (3 November) now covers declines and debit routing, so decline explainers are getting crowded. Our judgement is that Issue 01 should lean on what that piece does not do: a holdout-tested re-route compared across four markets, with the rule citations. The Mastercard token switchover, the UK scheme-fee governance rules and Nigeria's stablecoin rules have had no peer coverage that we could find.

## Expected vs happened

Nothing resolved this week. All nine open calls are too early to score, and the running Brier score stays at 0.0225 (n = 1).

- **Moving our way:** the EU payments rules are now formally with the Parliament, and the plenary is still pencilled in for 14 December.
- **Mixed:** the UPI charge has no date and faces a court challenge, but a 1 January start would still fall inside our window.
- **Flat:**
  - the settlement awaits its 16 November hearing;
  - no rehearing petition has been found in the 8th Circuit case;
  - no UK cap proposal;
  - no South African redraft;
  - no new Visa edition.
- **Our judgement:** moving the PSR into the FCA probably slows the UK cap.

**What we did not expect:** the PSR's scheme-fee directions and Mastercard's SCOF switchover. Both were public before run 2, and both were missed because we checked single pages rather than whole publication lists.

## Method changes

- **Sweep every regulator's full publication list, not single tracked pages, and chase every open gap each week.** Checking single pages is how we missed the PSR's scheme-fee directions and the SCOF article.
- **Never log a future or "proposed" event from a search summary alone.** It needs the primary page, otherwise we mark it unverified. We also now track whether last week's recommended edits have reached the drafts. Any edit still missing within 14 days of a send date gets escalated.
