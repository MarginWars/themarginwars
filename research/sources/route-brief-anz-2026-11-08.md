# Route Brief: Australia & NZ, 3DS/SCA, PSD3/PSR: source notes (checked 8 Nov 2026 PT)
All claims below are from pages opened 8 Nov 2026. Nothing claims The Margin Wars has run work in these markets.

## Australia (cards)
- Surcharging removed from 1 Oct 2026 on eftpos, Mastercard, Visa. RBA FAQs: https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/review-of-retail-payments-regulation/2026-03/conclusions-paper/faqs/index.html
- Lower domestic interchange caps from 1 Oct 2026; foreign-issued card cap from 1 Apr 2027; large-acquirer pass-through publication from 30 Jan 2027. RBA Conclusions Paper (Mar 2026), Impact and Implementation: https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/review-of-retail-payments-regulation/2026-03/conclusions-paper/impact-and-implementation.html (figures exist there: consumer credit 0.3%, debit 8c and 0.16%, foreign 1.0%; NOT shown on site, kept short)
- LCR / merchant-choice routing on dual-network debit; RBA expects acquirers to offer and promote; binary/threshold/dynamic. https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/debit-cards/least-cost-routing/ and backgrounder. Online/mobile-wallet LCR to be considered in a consultation "expected to commence in mid-2026" (Conclusions Paper ch.7). Flagged on site as "under further RBA consultation"; we have not confirmed it has started.
- ANZ, CBA, NAB, Westpac appear in RBA LCR implementation table (Sep 2026): https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/debit-cards/least-cost-routing/update-on-implementation.html
- AusPayNet CNP Fraud Mitigation Framework: SCA required once a merchant breaches fraud threshold (AUD 50k and 20 bps) for consecutive quarters. https://auspaynet.com.au/insights/initiatives/CNP-Fraud-Mitigation-Framework ; IAC Vol 7 v18 (effective 30 Jun 2026).
## Australia (instant)
- NPP and PayTo (AP+): https://www.auspayplus.com.au/solutions/npp , https://www.auspayplus.com.au/solutions/payto-for-businesses
## New Zealand
- Commerce Commission interchange regulation under Retail Payment System Act 2022: personal-card limits from 1 Dec 2025 (domestic) / 1 May 2026 (foreign); commercial credit card limits decided 22 Oct 2026, effective 22 Apr 2027. https://www.comcom.govt.nz/regulated-industries/projects/interchange-fee-regulation/
- Surcharging: no ban in force; ComCom guidance applies (https://www.comcom.govt.nz/regulated-industries/retail-payments/making-and-accepting-payments/). Government's intended ban stalled (press only, RNZ) so site says only "whether NZ surcharging rules change".
- Payments NZ governs rules for NZ core payment systems: https://www.paymentsnz.co.nz/
## UK / EU
- PSD3 / PSR: Council adopted first-reading position 29 Oct 2026 (Council doc 8543/26): https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-8543-2026-REV-1-ADD-1/en/pdf . Parliament second reading pending (secondary reporting says planned 14 Dec 2026; not stated on site). Not in force; PSD2 applies.
- SCA: EBA RTS under PSD2; FCA SCA page https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/strong-customer-authentication
- UK Finance: https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/about-us ; EPC manages SEPA schemes (existing site entry).
