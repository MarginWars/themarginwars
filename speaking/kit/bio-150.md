
Avi has worked across the US, UK/EU, Eurasia, South Africa, India and APAC. Earlier roles include VEON, Rand Merchant Bank, FNB and Accenture, and he co-founded Rentoza and Effectus. He has spoken at Snowflake Summit 2025 (keynote co-presenter), Money20/20 USA 2024, Fiserv Forum 2024 and MRC Las Vegas 2024, where he hosted "Marketplaces on the Rise". He writes The Margin Wars, payment tactics from the front lines, with his co-author, Anitha.
