## 2. Four signature talks

Every talk uses the same tactic card format as The Margin Wars Playbook (the Cross-Market Margin Playbook): **the move → mechanics → prerequisites → metrics and expected impact range → failure modes → owner → US / UK-EU / Africa / APAC differences → "what I'd do Monday"**. ### Talk 1 (flagship keynote): Payment tactics for margin wars

**Format.** 30-45 minute keynote, or 60 minutes with Q&A; can be cut to a 20-minute opening.

**Abstract.** Payment cost is one of the few lines in the P&L that operators can move in a quarter without a new product. This keynote walks through six specific moves, one at a time: re-routing a BIN range to a second acquirer, turning on network tokens for card-on-file, a retry schedule for soft declines, renegotiating interchange++ pass-through, a chargeback representment workflow, and where to place an FX markup. For each move Avi covers the mechanics, the prerequisites, the metric that proves it worked, an impact range, how it fails, and who has to own it. He also shows how the same move plays differently in the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC. The session closes with stablecoin settlement treated as a cost line against cards, FX and correspondent fees, not as a thesis. [WAR STORY: CONFIRM WITH AVI - two sanitised examples, one win and one failure mode]

**Target audience.** Heads of payments, CFOs and finance leaders at enterprise and cross-border merchants; product, pricing and partnership leads at acquirers and PSPs; investors who want payment cost as a value-creation lever.

**Best rooms.** Vendor-hosted customer events (Fiserv Forum and merchant-summit style), private corporate offsites, and conference main stages where the organiser wants a practitioner rather than a futurist.

### Talk 2: PayFac and AI - tactics for sub-merchant margin

**Format.** 30-minute talk, panel anchor or fireside. Builds on the Fiserv Forum 2024 "Future is PayFac and GenAI" panel.

**Abstract.** Becoming a payment facilitator changes who keeps the margin, and who carries the loss. The session works through the levers a platform actually controls: sub-merchant pricing (pass-through versus blended), onboarding and underwriting cost, reserve and payout policy, residual splits with the sponsor acquirer, and where AI is already useful (document checks, risk triage, support deflection) versus where it adds risk. Each lever comes with the metric to track and the failure mode to watch. [WAR STORY: CONFIRM WITH AVI]

**Target audience.** Software platforms and ISVs deciding between PayFac, PayFac-as-a-service and referral models; acquirer and processor partner teams; PayFac risk and finance leads.

### Talk 3: Marketplace payments and risk - payout, reserve and loss tactics

**Format.** 30-minute talk or hosted conversation. Builds on hosting "Marketplaces on the Rise: Payments and Risk" at MRC Las Vegas 2024.

**Abstract.** Marketplaces lose margin in three places: fraud and chargebacks, money held or paid out at the wrong time, and the cost of checking sellers at scale. This session takes one tactic in each place: payout timing and holds set by seller risk tier, rolling reserves sized to real exposure, and a chargeback representment workflow that decides which disputes to fight. For each one it covers the data needed, the owner, how it fails when it is set too loose or too tight, and how the rules change by market. [WAR STORY: CONFIRM WITH AVI]

**Target audience.** Marketplace and platform payments leads, trust and safety and risk teams, finance controllers, and MRC-style merchant risk practitioners.

### Talk 4: Merchant data and cloud data sharing - finding basis points in your own data

**Format.** 30-minute talk or co-presented case session. Builds on the Snowflake Summit 2025 keynote co-presented with Fiserv's VP Product, the Money20/20 USA 2024 session on merchant cloud data sharing with Snowflake, and the Data Cloud Now interview.


**Target audience.** Merchant payments, data and finance teams; processor and acquirer data-product leaders; data-platform partner teams.
