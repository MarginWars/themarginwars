Proposed session title: PayFac and AI: tactics for sub-merchant margin
Format: 30-minute session with a mandatory interactive element — a live, one-page scorecard the room fills in: who carries the loss, the four levers a platform controls, and the metric that proves each one. (Interactive element required; this is a workshop beat, not a slide-only talk.)
Level: Intermediate/advanced
Audience: FI and corporate treasury and payments operations; fintechs and platforms
Abstract:
Becoming a payment facilitator changes who keeps the margin and who carries the loss. This session works through the levers a platform actually controls: sub-merchant pricing (pass-through or blended), onboarding and underwriting cost, reserve and payout policy, and residual splits with the sponsor acquirer. It then separates where AI already earns its place, such as document checks, risk triage and support deflection, from where it adds risk. Every lever comes with the metric to track and the failure mode to watch, so platform, risk and finance teams leave with the same scorecard for embedded payments.
Takeaways: (1) price PayFac and embedded payments on who carries the loss, not on headline rate; (2) the four levers a platform controls, and the metric that proves each one; (3) where AI cuts underwriting and support cost today, and where it adds risk.
