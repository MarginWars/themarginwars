Proposed session title: Pay out later, lose less: payout, reserve and chargeback tactics for marketplaces
Format: 30-minute session (panel or co-presented talk)
Abstract:
Marketplaces lose margin in three places: fraud and chargebacks, money held or paid out at the wrong time, and the cost of checking sellers at scale. This session takes one tactic in each. First, payout timing and holds set by seller risk tier. Second, rolling reserves sized to real exposure rather than to habit. Third, a representment workflow that decides which chargebacks are worth fighting. For each tactic it covers the data you need, who owns it, how it fails when set too loose or too tight, and how the rules change by market, from the US and UK/EU to APAC.
Takeaways: (1) a risk-tiered payout and hold schedule you can test on one seller cohort; (2) how to size a rolling reserve to exposure, and when to release it; (3) a rule for which chargebacks to fight, priced against the cost of fighting.
