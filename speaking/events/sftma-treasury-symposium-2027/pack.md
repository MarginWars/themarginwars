Session Title: Six payment moves that win back margin in a quarter
Description (100-word limit):
Payment cost is one of the few P&L lines an operator can move inside a quarter without shipping a new product. This session works through six moves, one at a time: price on net revenue and take rate, not headline TPV; choose agent or merchant of record flow by flow; quote the FX markup per corridor, per order; move one corridor to local rails or account-to-account; price the agent channel; and map licence and tax exposure before a flow gets big. Each move comes with its mechanics, the owner, the proof metric and the rollback trigger, plus how it changes across the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC. No product pitch.
Learning objectives (4-bullet limit):
1. A one-page test for whether deals are priced on net margin or on volume.
2. How to find, per corridor, where the FX markup sits and who keeps it.
3. A move-by-move checklist: owner, proof metric and rollback trigger for each.
4. How those moves change when the corridor is US, UK/EU, Africa or APAC.
