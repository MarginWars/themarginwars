Proposed session (for the email; Talk 1, merchant-cost fit):
Title: Six payment moves that win back margin in a quarter
Abstract:
Payment cost is one of the few P&L lines an operator can move inside a quarter without shipping a new product. This session works through six moves, one at a time: price on net revenue and take rate, not headline TPV; choose agent or merchant of record flow by flow; quote the FX markup per corridor, per order; move one corridor to local rails or account-to-account; price the agent channel; and map licence and tax exposure before a flow gets big. Each move comes with its mechanics, the owner, the proof metric and the rollback trigger. No product pitch.
