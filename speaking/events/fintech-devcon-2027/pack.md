Session description/abstract (≤800 characters; this one is about 690):
Most merchants already hold the data to find lost margin, but it sits in their processor's systems, not their own. This roadblock talk covers how direct processor-to-merchant data sharing, with no file exports and no copies, unblocked three tactics: approval-rate analysis by BIN and issuer to decide routing, decline-code analysis that feeds a soft-decline retry schedule, and a line-by-line check of fees against an interchange++ statement. It covers what we asked for, who owned the analysis, what broke, and the governance traps.
