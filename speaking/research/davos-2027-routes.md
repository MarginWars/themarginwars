## Stablecoin talk angle (draft, no employer or client named)

FLAG — do not submit this talk to a public stage until Avi explicitly OKs the exception. talks.md and 09_Speaker_Kit.md keep "stablecoin settlement as a cost line" as closed-door only, after a conflict check. Avi asked for a stablecoin angle for Davos; this draft is ready, but it is not cleared for a public form or a public email until that OK (and the conflict check) is on the record. Do not name any employer or client.

Title: Price the rail, not the token: stablecoin settlement as a cost line for merchants and PSPs
Abstract: Stablecoin settlement only helps a merchant or a PSP if it beats the rails already on the cost table — interchange, FX markup, correspondent fees and payout timing — corridor by corridor. This session treats stablecoin settlement as a cost and margin line, not a theme. It works through three moves: put issuance, on- and off-ramp, FX and payout cost next to card and account-to-account alternatives for one corridor; decide who carries the float, the reserve and the operational failure; and set a rollback trigger before volume moves. Each move is shown across the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC, including where licence, banking access or off-ramp liquidity makes the cheaper rail the wrong one. No product pitch.
Takeaways: (1) a one-corridor cost table that puts stablecoin settlement next to cards, local rails and correspondent payout; (2) who carries float, reserve and operational failure when settlement moves off traditional rails; (3) a go/no-go and rollback test before a corridor is switched.

Best-fit rooms for this angle, if Avi clears the public-talk exception: The Hub Davos speaker form (ready-to-file draft in `11_Speaker_Submissions/hub-davos-2027_FINAL.md`); Blockchain Central Davos (email pitch); DavosWeb3 (email pitch). Official WEF stages are invitation-only. Official WEF programmes that already discuss digital assets (not application routes into the January meeting) include The Future of Blockchain and Digital Assets (https://initiatives.weforum.org/blockchain/home) and the Digital Currency Governance Consortium. WEF also ran a 2026 Annual Meeting session, "Where Are We on Stablecoins?" That is precedent for the topic inside the official programme, not an open 2027 CFP.
