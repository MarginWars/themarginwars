TPV is applause. Net revenue is the paycheck.

Headlines love volume. Renewals fight over the rate. Neither tells you whether a deal makes money. A book can grow and still get thinner: volume up, net revenue flat. That's mix leaking margin.

What I would check on Monday:

1. Split volume by product line: branded or high-margin vs unbranded or processing-only.
2. For each line, compute net revenue per dollar of volume: what you bill, minus interchange, scheme fees, refunds, disputes and FX.
3. Check whether growth is concentrating in the lowest-margin line.
4. If it is, attach value to that volume (fraud tools, payouts, tokenized credentials) or reprice it. Don't let processing-only growth set the take rate for the whole book.

Margin rarely disappears in one big number. It goes in a hundred small ones: downgrades, unchecked scheme fees, hidden FX, soft declines, lazy routing.

If you could put one number next to TPV on every board slide, which would it be? Net take rate, cost per approved order, or something better?
