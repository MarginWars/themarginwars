## About

Nobody loses margin on the rate. You lose it in the line items nobody owns.

I run enterprise payments product at a global payments network. I've spent 19 years in payments, across the US, UK/EU, Eurasia, South Africa, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Australia & NZ, Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong. I co-founded Rentoza and Effectus.

Field record: turned a loss-making acquiring platform profitable, a billion-dollar transaction-margin swing. Co-inventor on a pending US patent for LLM-orchestrated deal pricing and margin forecasting.

The Margin Wars is where Anitha and I write down what we've learned, one tactic at a time. Eight years working side by side. Every tactic gets the same card:
- the move, step by step
- prerequisites
- the proof metric, with a sourced or labelled impact range
- failure modes and the rollback
- who owns it
- how it plays in the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC
- what we would do on Monday

The fronts: interchange downgrades, scheme fees, FX by corridor, acceptance and soft declines, routing on all-in cost per approved order, and cross-border fees on local-eligible payments.

Where I've made the case: Snowflake Summit 2025 (keynote co-presenter), Money20/20 USA 2024, Fiserv Forum 2024 ("Future is PayFac and GenAI") and MRC Las Vegas 2024 (host, "Marketplaces on the Rise: Payments and Risk").

Ways to work together:
- Margin Teardown: for when you know you're leaking but not where
- Campaign Sprint: for one decision you can't afford to get wrong
- Board sessions and workshops, and retained advisory
- Talks: keynotes, moderating, closed-door sessions and workshops

The Margin Wars Briefing: one tactic a month you can run on Monday. Free, by invitation. Message me, or request an invitation at themarginwars.com.
