Fighting over "the rate" is noise. The margin is in the lines nobody reads.

Episode 2 of The Margin Wars, Close Quarters, is built around that.

An illustrative enterprise fee statement: 12 lines, three of them wrong. Not a real merchant. The patterns are.

A "non-qualified" surcharge on chip card-present sales. A cross-currency fee on domestic sales. A PCI non-compliance fee charged with a valid AOC on file. None of them in the headline rate.

The move I would make: stop negotiating from range. Map every statement line to the rule that allows it. Hunt those three patterns first, and put a name next to every flag.

Which line on your statement has nobody's name next to it?

Play the episode and find the leaks yourself: link in the first comment.
