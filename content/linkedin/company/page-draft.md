## About (under 2,000 characters)
> The Margin Wars is a payments brand from Avi and Anitha, two payments operators who write and speak about one thing: winning back payment margin.
>
> We cover tactics, not themes. Each one is a specific move on a front where payment margin is being fought now: reporting net revenue instead of headline TPV, choosing agent or merchant of record flow by flow, quoting the FX markup per corridor, moving corridors to local rails and account-to-account payments, repricing enterprise deals on net margin, PayFac and embedded payments pricing, mapping licences, scheme rules and tax duties before a flow gets big, stablecoin settlement and float as a cost line, and agentic commerce as one front among them: pricing the agent channel, the ledger for money flowing back to the agent, user mandates, and an agent-ready checkout on your own terms.
>
> Every tactic is written the same way: the move, the prerequisites, the metrics and the impact range, the failure modes and the rollback, the owner, and a cross-continent campaign column for the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC.
>
>
> Ways in: The Margin Wars Briefing (invite-only), closed-door Working Sessions, Margin Teardowns, advisory, and talks. Recent stages include Snowflake Summit 2025 (keynote), Money20/20 USA 2024, Fiserv Forum 2024 and MRC Las Vegas 2024.
>
> Views are our own and do not represent any employer or client.
