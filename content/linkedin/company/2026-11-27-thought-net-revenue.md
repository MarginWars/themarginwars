TPV is applause. Net revenue is the paycheck.

Headlines love volume. Renewals fight over the rate. Neither tells you whether a deal makes money.

The Monday move:
1. Take your biggest enterprise deal.
2. Rebuild it on one page: what you bill, minus interchange, minus scheme fees, minus refunds, disputes and FX costs. That's net revenue.
3. Divide by volume. That's your real take rate.
4. Do the same for the next nine deals. Rank all ten by net take rate, not by volume.
5. Reprice from the bottom of that list, on net margin, not on volume.

Margin rarely disappears in one big number. It goes in a hundred small ones: downgrades, unchecked scheme fees, hidden FX, soft declines, lazy routing.

So, payments people: if you could put one number next to TPV on every board slide, which would it be? Net take rate, cost per approved order, or something better? And which line most often turns a "big" deal into a thin one?
