Nobody loses margin on the rate. You lose it in the line items nobody owns.

The Margin Wars is open. We're Avi and Anitha, eight years working side by side. We find the line items eating your margin, rank them by basis points, and give each one an owner.

Not one big number. A hundred small ones. Six fronts, each with a move:

Interchange: sort the qualification report by reason code. Fix the biggest bucket first.
Scheme fees: map each line to its rule. Flag what you shouldn't pay.
FX: quote it per corridor, per order. Then decide where it sits.
Acceptance: soft vs hard declines, smart retries, network tokens. Price each lost sale against the fee.
Routing: route on all-in cost per approved order.
Cross-border: test one corridor, local acquiring or A2A vs a control, on cost, approvals and settlement days.

Every tactic is written the same way, and shown for the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC.
