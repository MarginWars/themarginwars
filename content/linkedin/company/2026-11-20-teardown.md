Bring one bleeding number: cost per order, approvals, chargebacks or FX. We scope around it.

That's the Margin Teardown, for when you know you're leaking but not where. We find the line items eating your margin, rank them by basis points, and give each one an owner.

How it runs:
1. Scope. One call to name the number. A fixed fee, one deliverable.
2. Clear the ground. Conflict check before anything confidential. Conflicts, we decline.
3. Work quietly. NDA as standard. Client names only with written permission.
4. Hand over. Ranked moves, an owner each, and the metric that proves it.

Helpful to include when you get in touch: your organisation, role and markets, and the number you want to move.

We take few engagements at a time.
