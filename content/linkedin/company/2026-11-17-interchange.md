Front 01: Interchange. The rate you negotiated isn't the rate you pay if your transactions keep downgrading.

The problem: downgrades from missing Level 2/3 data, wrong card-present flags, no AVS, late capture. None of them arrives as a line called "downgrade".

The move: sort the qualification report by reason code. Fix the biggest bucket first.

Three checks before you start:
- Which downgrades are you paying for? Read the report by reason code: L2/3 data, card-present, AVS, capture.
- Who owns the fix? Data fields, capture timing and the report often sit with different teams. No owner, no move.
- What would prove it worked? The share of volume in the qualified category, tracked by reason code, month on month.

Monday version: pull last month's qualification report from each acquirer, sort by reason code, and name one owner for the top bucket.
