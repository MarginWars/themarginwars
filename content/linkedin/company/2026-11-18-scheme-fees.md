Front 02: Scheme fees. Dozens of network fee lines per acquirer. Nobody checks them.

They're small, they're technical, and they sit between interchange and the acquirer's markup, so each team assumes another one is reading them.

The move: map each line to its rule. Flag what you shouldn't pay.

How to start:
- Export one month of scheme fee lines from each acquirer.
- Next to each line, write the network rule that triggers it.
- Any line you can't map to a rule, or that shouldn't apply to your flows, goes on a list for the acquirer.

How it fails: nobody owns the list after the first call. Give it an owner and a monthly check, or the same lines come back.

That's the pattern across every front: not one big number, a hundred small ones. Together, they're the margin.
