Fighting over "the rate" is noise. The margin is in the lines.

Lesson 2 of The Margin Wars: Close Quarters. Six slides:

→ The tactic: stop negotiating from range. Map every statement line to the rule that allows it.
→ The war story: one illustrative enterprise fee statement, 12 lines. Three were wrong.
→ The three leaks: a "non-qualified" surcharge on chip card-present sales, a cross-currency fee on domestic sales, and a PCI non-compliance fee charged with a valid AOC on file. None of them in the headline rate.
→ The Monday move: pull every line, write the rule next to it, hunt those three patterns first, give every flag an owner.

Illustrative statement, not a real merchant. The patterns are real enough to check this week.

Which line on your statement has nobody's name next to it?
