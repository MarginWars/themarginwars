## 4. About text
> **The Margin Wars** is a private letter from Avi and Anitha, two payments operators, for senior payments and finance leaders.
>
> We write about tactics, not themes. Each post covers one move: how it works step by step, what you need in place first, the metrics and the impact range, the failure modes and the rollback, who owns it, and how it plays in each market through a cross-continent campaign column covering the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC.
>
>
> Subscriptions are by approval only. Posts are for subscribers, and we ask that you don't forward them. Views are our own and do not represent any employer or client. Stories are shared with details removed or changed so that no employer, client or customer can be identified.
