**Subtitle:** Payments tactics from the front lines.

Nobody loses margin on the rate. You lose it in the line items nobody owns.

We're Avi and Anitha, two payments operators. Eight years working side by side. Avi works on margin and platform; Anitha builds acquiring businesses market by market. Between us we've worked payments from the US and UK/EU to South Africa, India, Australia & NZ, Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong.

The Margin Wars is where we write down what we've learned, one tactic at a time.

## What we believe

- **The rate is the wrong fight.** The money is in downgrades, scheme lines and unquoted FX.
- **Net, not gross.** Volume flatters. Price on net revenue and take rate.
- **Every corridor is its own front.** FX, rails and rules change at every border.
- **Name the owner.** No owner, no metric, no move.
- **Know where it breaks.** Every tactic ships with a rollback.

## What you'll get here

Tactics, not themes. Each tactic is written the same way:

1. The move, step by step
2. Prerequisites
3. The proof metric, with a sourced or labelled impact range
4. Failure modes and the rollback
5. Who owns it
6. How it plays in the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC
7. What we would do on Monday

No news round-ups, no hot takes, no sponsors, and no employer or client data. Stories are shared with details changed so that no employer, client or customer can be identified.
