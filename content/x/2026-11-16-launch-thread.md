Nobody loses margin on the rate. You lose it in the line items nobody owns.

The Margin Wars is open: payments tactics from the front lines, by Avi and Anitha.

Where margin goes missing, and what to do about it:
---
Not one big number. A hundred small ones: downgrades, unchecked scheme fees, hidden FX, soft declines, lazy routing, avoidable cross-border fees.

Together, they're the margin.
---
Interchange: sort the qualification report by reason code. Fix the biggest bucket first.

Scheme fees: map each line to its rule. Flag what you shouldn't pay.

FX: quote it per corridor, per order. Then decide where it sits.
---
Acceptance: soft vs hard declines. Price each lost sale against the fee.

Routing: route on all-in cost per approved order.

Cross-border: one corridor, local acquiring or A2A vs a control.

Each shown for the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC.
