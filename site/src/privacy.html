Privacy

What this site keeps, why, and how to reach us about it.

Updated 25 November 2026

The short version

This notice covers themarginwars.com. We use the information you send to answer you and run the site. We do not set our own cookies or use third-party advertising pixels. Some features use storage in your browser; the forms and the assistant use Cloudflare services. Those details are below.

What stays in your browser

First-party, cookieless measurement uses sessionStorage for this tab's first and latest campaign source, medium, campaign, content, referring site and landing page. It is not a persistent visitor ID. The site sends page, campaign and event information to our own endpoint, which keeps daily aggregate counts of visits, CTA and outbound-link clicks, form sends and game shares, not an IP address, user agent or visitor ID in those counters.

Games save progress and streaks on your device with localStorage. Research feeds save your market and rail preferences and last-visit state in browser storage. You can clear these through your browser's site-data controls; doing so also clears saved game progress and preferences. Closing the tab ends its session storage.

When you send a form

For contact and Briefing requests, we store your email, selected topic, any message, name or company you choose to give, submission time and site host in Cloudflare D1. We also store campaign attribution if present, a hashed IP value for abuse controls, your browser's user-agent string, and notification status. Name, company and the one-line message are optional.

If you ask the site assistant to pass a question to the team, we keep the name, email, company if provided, question, recent conversation turns you submit with it, page, time and any campaign attribution in Cloudflare KV. The team receives a notification of form requests so we can respond. An automated receipt is not sent to the visitor by these form endpoints.

Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded when you interact with a form to help prevent spam. Cloudflare processes technical browser and network information for this check. Rate limiting also uses IP-related information to protect the endpoints. Please do not submit payment-card numbers, passwords or confidential employer or client data.

The site assistant

If you ask the assistant a question, your question and the recent conversation context are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce a reply using the site's material. The conversation is held in the page while you use it. If you choose to send a question to the team, the submitted question and recent context are saved as described above.

Analytics and other services

The site uses the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon for traffic and performance measurement, alongside our first-party aggregate event counts. The beacon is not an advertising pixel. Cloudflare also hosts and delivers the site and processes technical request information to do so.

Fonts are served by this site. A YouTube video is loaded only when you choose to play it; that connects your browser to YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed. Share buttons open the service you select, such as LinkedIn or X, and do not load their tracking pixels here. External links, including the Briefing on Substack, take you to services with their own privacy notices.

Keeping and removing information

We keep submitted requests so the team can respond and follow up. We have not set an automatic deletion period. Contact us if you want to know what we hold, or want it corrected or removed; we may need enough detail to find your request.

For a privacy question or request, use Contact and put “Privacy” in your message, or email us directly. We will update this page when these practices change.
