How we handle history

We cite museum and historical sources and keep claims modest. Historians disagree about how much Shaka originated and how much he developed from earlier practice in Dingiswayo's Mthethwa army. The earliest written accounts were by European traders and are widely treated as unreliable. So we say "associated with", not "invented by". Sources: South African History Online; Ditsong Museums of South Africa; summary of the historiography. Academic reading: Carolyn Hamilton, Terrific Majesty: The Powers of Shaka Zulu and the Limits of Historical Invention (Harvard University Press, 1998), on how images of Shaka were made and contested; and Renier H. van der Merwe and Innocent Pikirayi, "The organisation and layout of Zulu military homesteads (amakhanda)" (University of Pretoria repository), on the amabutho age-regiment system.
