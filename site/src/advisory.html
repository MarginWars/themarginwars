02

Ways to work together

Margin Teardown

For when you know you're leaking but not where. Two days on your own payments data. We go through net revenue and pricing, merchant-of-record exposure, FX and local rails, disputes, tax, and agent channels where you have them. You get a ranked 90-day list of moves, each with an owner. $17,500.

Campaign Sprint

For one decision you can't afford to get wrong. Six to eight days on a single question. One example is how to acquire and price in a new market. Another is whether stablecoin settlement beats your current rail on all-in cost. $30,000-$40,000.

Board sessions and workshops

For when the leadership team needs to agree on what matters. A closed-door briefing, or a half-day or full-day workshop, on the margin levers in front of your board. $500 per person per day.

Retained advisory

For the long campaign. A defined programme of support while the moves land, with the Playbook refreshed every quarter for your markets. Minimum 16 hours a month. By proposal.

03

Questions clients bring us

  • Are we pricing enterprise deals on net margin, or on headline TPV?
  • Should we be merchant of record in this market, and what does that do to net revenue versus headline TPV?
  • Which licences, scheme rules and tax duties land on us once the flow gets big?
  • What does an agent-originated order really cost us, next to a direct one?
  • Which disputes are worth fighting, and what should the workflow look like?
  • Where should the FX markup sit, and when does local acquiring pay for itself?
  • Does stablecoin settlement beat our current cross-border rail on all-in cost?
  • Which of our interchange downgrades can be fixed with better data at checkout?
  • Which scheme fee lines on our statement can't we explain?
  • Is our routing sending volume to the cheapest acquirer, or just the usual one?

What you leave with

Every engagement ends in the same four things: the moves, ranked; an owner for each; the metric that proves it worked; and the early warning that tells you to roll it back.

04

How engagements run

  1. 01

    Scope. A short call to name the number you want to move. Then a written proposal with a fixed fee and one clear deliverable.

  2. 02

    Clear the ground. We run a conflict check before any confidential detail changes hands. If a matter conflicts with an existing commitment, we decline it.

  3. 03

    Work quietly. A confidentiality agreement is standard. We use client names only with written permission.

  4. 04

    Hand over. You get the list of moves, the owner for each one, and the metric that will show whether it worked.
