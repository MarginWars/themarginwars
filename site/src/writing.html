The Margin Wars Briefing

A private monthly briefing for about 150 people who own a payment cost, approval, loss or FX number. Each issue is one tactic. It is free, and you join by invitation.

Who it is for

  • Who owns it

  • What we would do on Monday

Each issue is one tactic.

Excerpt: Tactic card 01

Price the agent channel before it prices you

The move. Put every agent-originated order into its own channel: the fee paid to the agent, interchange, scheme and acquirer costs, refunds, claw-backs and disputes in one cost-per-order line, measured against a holdout of direct orders, so the cost of the channel is measured, not assumed.

Before you start. Decide who is merchant of record for each flow, and check the principal-versus-agent accounting with finance. Flag agent-initiated transactions at the checkout, and set a separate dispute reason for "agent bought the wrong thing".

Cross-continent campaign column. In the US, card rails and marketplace-facilitator tax rules set the first terms. In the UK and EU, SCA and consumer-rights rules decide who carries the refund. In APAC, local wallets and schemes decide whether the agent can pay at all. In Africa, mobile money and FX access decide whether the order settles.

The full card, with metrics, failure modes, owners and Monday actions, goes to readers. Excerpts are published about once a quarter.
