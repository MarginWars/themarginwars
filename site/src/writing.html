Excerpt: Tactic card 01

Price the agent channel before it prices you

The move. Give agent orders their own channel: agent fee, interchange, scheme, refunds and disputes in one cost-per-order line, against a direct holdout.

Before you start. Set MoR per flow. Flag agent transactions at checkout. Add a dispute reason: "agent bought the wrong thing".

Cross-continent campaign column. US: card rails and facilitator tax. UK/EU: SCA and refund rules. APAC: local wallets. Africa: mobile money and FX access.
