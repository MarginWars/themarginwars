What we believe

  1. The rate is the wrong fight. The money is in downgrades, scheme lines and unquoted FX.
  2. Net, not gross. Volume flatters. Price on net revenue and take rate.
  3. Every corridor is its own front. FX, rails and rules change at every border.
  4. Name the owner. No owner, no metric, no move.
  5. Know where it breaks. Every tactic ships with a rollback.
