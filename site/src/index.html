Specific moves, not trends.

The operator's playbook for winning back payment margin, one tactic at a time, in every market you sell in.

Discuss a margin problemRequest an invitation to the Briefing

We are Anitha and Avi, co-authors of The Margin Wars. Anitha is a payments and product executive with 15+ years across PayPal, Braintree and Stripe, in enterprise payments, acquiring and partnerships. Avi is a payments and product executive with 15+ years across PayPal, VEON and Rand Merchant Bank, in enterprise payments, pricing and data. Together we show payments and finance leaders specific moves, not trends: net revenue versus headline TPV, merchant-of-record and buy-sell structures, FX spread and local rails per corridor, PayFac, embedded and enterprise pricing, disputes, the licence, scheme and tax exposure that follows big flows, stablecoin settlement and float, and agentic checkout, and how each one plays in the US, UK/EU, Africa and APAC.

Four ways in

The Margin Wars Briefing

Invite-only and monthly. One tactic per issue, for about 150 senior payments operators.

Working Sessions

Closed-door sessions for 8-12 peers, each on one lever, held around major conferences in different regions.

Margin Teardown

Two days on your own data, ending in a prioritised 90-day list of moves.

Advisory

Sprints and retained advice for cross-continent expansion campaigns and stablecoin cost decisions.
