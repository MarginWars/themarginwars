Signature talks

Payment tactics for margin wars

Flagship keynote, 30-45 minutes. Six specific moves, one at a time: reporting net revenue instead of headline TPV, choosing merchant of record flow by flow, quoting the FX markup corridor by corridor, moving a corridor to local rails, pricing the agent channel, and mapping the licence, scheme and tax exposure that follows a big flow. Each comes with mechanics, metrics, failure modes, owners and the differences across cross-continent campaigns.

PayFac and AI: tactics for sub-merchant margin

Sub-merchant pricing, onboarding and underwriting cost, reserves and residual splits, and where AI already helps. Builds on the Fiserv Forum 2024 PayFac and GenAI panel.

Marketplace payments and risk: payout, reserve and loss tactics

Payout timing and holds by seller risk tier, rolling reserves, and which chargebacks to fight. Builds on hosting the MRC Las Vegas 2024 marketplaces session.

Merchant data and cloud data sharing: finding basis points in your own data

Using direct processor-to-merchant data sharing to see the true cost of each order: scheme and tax lines, FX spread, disputes, refunds and, where agents sell, agent fees in one view, by channel and by corridor. Builds on Snowflake Summit 2025 and Money20/20 USA 2024.

Also available as a closed-door briefing: stablecoin settlement as a cost line for acquirers and merchants, offered only after a conflict check.
